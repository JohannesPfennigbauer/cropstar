Cropstar is not meant to make decisions for you. It is meant to make relationships visible so you can make your own decisions better. This blog is the first part of that.

## What this is about

Two themes will run throughout:

- **Regenerative practice** – what cover crops, mixed cropping, manure and reduced tillage do in the soil, and why.
- **Crop modelling** – how a model recalculates growth, water and nutrients, where it gets things wrong, and what its uncertainty means.

## How we write

Every number gets a source. Where we don't know something precisely, we say so. And some posts will be interactive: sliders you can turn to explore cause and effect yourself.

Guest posts from practice, advisory services and research are welcome – in English, too.
